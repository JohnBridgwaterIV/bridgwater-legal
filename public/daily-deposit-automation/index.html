Application information page

Daily Deposit Automation is Bridgwater Automotive LLC's private accounting tool for authorized internal users. It is being developed to reconcile payment and payout activity, transaction fees, and bank-deposit records in an authorized QuickBooks Online company. Production use requires a QuickBooks administrator's authorization.

Public legal pages do not provide access to accounting records or authorize the application to connect to QuickBooks.
